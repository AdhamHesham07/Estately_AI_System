JLL Egypt Cairo Real Estate Market Overview - 2024 Summary

### Key Insights from JLL's 2024 Cairo Real Estate Reporting:

1. Residential Sector:
The residential sector began 2024 with robust activity. Significant increases were observed in secondary market sales prices, with reports noting substantial surges in highly demanded areas like 6th of October City, Sheikh Zayed City, and New Cairo. The market saw a major focus on master-planned community developments, with thousands of units completed throughout the year. The trend shows buyers heavily preferring gated compounds with integrated amenities over standalone buildings.

2. Office Market:
The office sector maintained a positive long-term outlook, driven by consistent corporate demand for high-quality, Grade-A spaces, particularly in East Cairo (New Cairo) and West Cairo (Sheikh Zayed). Stability in currency exchange rates was noted as a key factor in boosting investor confidence and providing clarity on long-term leasing terms for multinational corporations.

3. Hospitality & Tourism:
The hospitality sector showed extreme resilience and was poised for recovery and expansion. This growth is heavily supported by government tourism initiatives, infrastructure expansions, and increased foreign direct investment (FDI). Hotel occupancy rates in Greater Cairo have steadily climbed, prompting developers to integrate branded residences and boutique hotels into their master plans.

4. Retail Market:
The retail market faced challenges throughout the year, primarily due to inflationary pressures limiting consumer spending and causing some project delays. However, the entry and expansion of local Egyptian brands provided essential support to mall occupancy rates, filling gaps left by international franchises.

5. Investment Trends & Valuations:
Due to inflation, real estate remains the primary safe-haven asset for Egyptian investors. Properties in prime locations (New Cairo, North Coast, Sheikh Zayed) are seeing strong capital appreciation. However, buyers are increasingly price-sensitive, looking for flexible payment plans (installments over 7-10 years) directly from developers (Off-Plan) rather than upfront cash purchases in the secondary market.
