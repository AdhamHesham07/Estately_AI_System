# EGYPT REAL ESTATE TIER-1 DEVELOPER PROFILES (2024 UPDATE)

1. Emaar Misr
- Profile: The ultra-luxury standard in Egypt. A subsidiary of the UAE-based Emaar Properties.
- Flagship Projects: Marassi (North Coast), Uptown Cairo (Central Cairo), Mivida (New Cairo), Cairo Gate (Sheikh Zayed).
- Reputation: Known for impeccable landscaping, extremely high-quality finishes, and elite community management.
- Investment Angle: Very high initial entry price. Secondary market resale value is exceptionally strong. Emaar properties are considered liquid assets due to massive brand loyalty.

2. SODIC (Sixth of October Development and Investment Company)
- Profile: A massive, highly respected developer with a strong focus on community, modern architecture, and upper-middle to luxury segments. Acquired by Aldar Properties (UAE).
- Flagship Projects: SODIC West, Allegria, Villette (New Cairo), October Plaza, The Estates.
- Reputation: Excellent track record for on-time delivery and strong community services (Club S).
- Investment Angle: SODIC offers consistent, steady capital appreciation. Very popular among expat renters, making them excellent choices for buy-to-let investors looking for strong rental yields in New Cairo and Zayed.

3. Palm Hills Developments (PHD)
- Profile: One of the oldest and largest land banks in Egypt. Specializes in massive master-planned cities.
- Flagship Projects: Palm Hills October, Palm Hills New Cairo, Badya (Creative City), Hacienda (North Coast).
- Reputation: Known for sprawling golf courses, family-friendly environments, and vast green spaces.
- Investment Angle: Badya in West Cairo is seen as a major long-term growth play due to its massive scale and integration with the new infrastructure networks.

4. Talaat Moustafa Group (TMG)
- Profile: The pioneer of the "City within a City" concept in Egypt. Targets the massive middle to upper-middle class.
- Flagship Projects: Madinaty, Al Rehab, Celia (New Capital), Noor City.
- Reputation: Unmatched in scale. Madinaty operates like an independent municipality. 
- Investment Angle: Very high liquidity. Units in Madinaty and Al Rehab are the easiest to rent out or resell in the country due to massive local demand and fully operational infrastructure.

5. Ora Developers (Naguib Sawiris)
- Profile: A relatively newer player but backed by billionaire Naguib Sawiris. Focuses heavily on lifestyle, sports, and ultra-modern designs.
- Flagship Projects: ZED El Sheikh Zayed, ZED East (New Cairo), Silversands (North Coast).
- Reputation: ZED projects are famous for their massive central parks, sports clubs, and integrated commercial strips (ZED Strip). Delivery is fully finished with ACs and kitchen cabinets, which is rare in Egypt.
- Investment Angle: Fully finished units save investors the massive headache of finishing costs (which are rising due to inflation), allowing for immediate rental generation upon delivery.
