# EGYPT REGIONAL REAL ESTATE ANALYSIS (2024)

## EAST CAIRO: New Cairo & The New Administrative Capital (NAC)
- **New Cairo (The 5th Settlement / Tagamoa):** The most established premium market in Egypt right now. It is fully operational with top international schools (AUC), mega-malls (Cairo Festival City), and major corporate headquarters (Sector 90). 
- **Mostakbal City:** The "bridge" between New Cairo and the New Capital. It is an emerging mega-city focusing heavily on green spaces and sustainability. It offers slightly lower price-per-square-meter compared to New Cairo but promises high capital appreciation as it connects to the NAC.
- **The New Administrative Capital (NAC):** A massive government-backed project. While residential occupancy is still growing, the commercial and administrative sectors are booming as ministries and banks relocate. It is a long-term investment play (5-10 years) for massive capital gains.

## WEST CAIRO: Sheikh Zayed & 6th of October
- **Sheikh Zayed City:** The premium hub of the West. It is smaller and more exclusive than New Cairo. It has seen a massive resurgence in demand due to infrastructure upgrades (new highways connecting it directly to Central Cairo and the North Coast) and the launch of new mega-projects by Emaar (Cairo Gate) and Ora (ZED).
- **New Zayed / October Extension:** As central Zayed runs out of land, developers are moving to the "New Zayed" extension. This area is currently dominated by massive off-plan projects (SODIC's The Estates, Emaar's Belle Vie) and represents a prime opportunity for ground-floor entry pricing.
- **6th of October City:** A massive, diverse city. The traditional areas are heavily populated and offer strong, consistent rental yields for middle-class housing.

## THE NORTH COAST (Sahel)
- **Profile:** Historically a summer holiday destination (July-August), but massive government infrastructure and the monumental Ras El Hekma deal (UAE investment) are transforming it into a year-round destination.
- **Investment Reality:** Properties here are extremely expensive but offer the highest short-term rental yields in the country during the summer months. Prime developers: Emaar (Marassi), Hassan Allam (Swan Lake), Ora (Silversands).
